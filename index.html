A simple admin tool for running a customer loyalty-points program — earn, redeem, and correct, right from your phone or laptop. No POS integration required.

the problem

Paper punch cards don't come back with data.

  • Stamped cards get lost, forgotten, or forged — there's no record behind them.
  • No way to know who your repeat customers actually are, or how often they come back.
  • A spreadsheet doesn't stop someone entering the same redemption twice.
  • When something's wrong, there's no clean way to correct it without erasing the history.

the solution

One tool. Three things it does well.

Earn

Log a bill amount and a bill number, credit points at your own ₹-per-point ratio — instantly.

Redeem

Look a customer up by phone, spend their balance — with an optional OTP so only they can approve it.

Correct

Mistyped a bill? Reverse any transaction, with a full record of who did it and when.
